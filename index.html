+Q Clips

+Q Clips is a short-form video brand. We publish short, subtitled clips of podcasts, creators, entertainment, music and gaming in English, mostly as part of official clipping campaigns run by the creators and brands themselves.

Find us on TikTok @masqclips10, Instagram @masqclips and YouTube @masqclips.

+Q Clips Publisher

+Q Clips Publisher is our internal tool. It is used only by the +Q Clips team, on our own accounts, to:

  • upload our own edited videos to our YouTube channel, TikTok account and Instagram account through their official APIs;
  • read the view counts of the videos we published, to know how each clip performs;
  • store and retrieve our own video files in our own Google Drive.

It is not offered to the public and does not collect data from viewers or other users. See our Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.

Paid clips are always disclosed as advertising (#ad) and with each platform's branded-content or paid-partnership setting.
