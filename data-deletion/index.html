Data deletion instructions

+Q Clips Publisher only holds data about the +Q Clips accounts themselves (access tokens and view counts of our own videos). It holds no data about other users.

To delete it:

  1. Revoke the app's access from the account: Google at myaccount.google.com/permissions; TikTok in Settings and privacy → Security → Apps and services; Instagram in Settings → Apps and websites.
  2. Send an email to masqclips@gmail.com with the subject "Data deletion". We delete the stored tokens and statistics within 30 days and confirm by email.
