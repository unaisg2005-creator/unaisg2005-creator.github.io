Privacy Policy

Effective date: 23 November 2026

This policy explains how +Q Clips ("we") handles data in +Q Clips Publisher, the internal tool we use to publish videos to our own social media accounts. The tool is used only by the +Q Clips team and only on accounts we own.

What data the tool accesses

  • Google / YouTube (scopes youtube.upload and youtube.readonly): uploading videos to our YouTube channel and reading the status and view counts of those videos. Scope drive.file: only the files that the tool itself creates in our Google Drive (our own video files). The tool cannot see any other file in the Drive.
  • TikTok (scopes user.info.basic, video.publish, video.list): our account's basic profile, publishing our videos and reading their view counts.
  • Instagram (permissions instagram_business_basic, instagram_business_content_publish, instagram_business_manage_insights): our account's basic profile, publishing our Reels and reading their view counts.

We do not access data from any other person, viewer or account.

How we use it

Only to publish our own videos and to measure how they perform (views per video), so we can report results to the campaigns we take part in. We do not use the data for advertising, profiling or any other purpose.

Storage and security

Access tokens are stored encrypted as secrets in our private GitHub repository and are only used by our own automated jobs. Video statistics (video link, date, view count) are stored in our private repository and in our own Google Sheet.

Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share this data with anyone. The only exception is the public link of a published video, which we send to the clipping campaign it belongs to.

Google API Services

+Q Clips Publisher's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

The tool uses YouTube API Services. By using it you also agree to the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Retention and deletion

Tokens are kept while the tool is in use and deleted when an account is disconnected. Statistics are kept for up to 24 months. Access can be revoked at any time:

  • Google: myaccount.google.com/permissions (also revokes access through Google's security settings page)
  • TikTok: Settings and privacy → Security → Apps and services
  • Instagram: Settings → Apps and websites

See also Data deletion instructions.

Children

The tool is not directed to children and does not process children's data.

Changes

We will update this page if anything changes, with a new effective date.
